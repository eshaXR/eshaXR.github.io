Multisensory Design · Spatial Audio · Haptics
Esha Mahendran
Multisensory Experience Designer · Human Factors PhD Candidate · Virginia Tech

I design how intelligent systems communicate without a screen. My work combines spatial audio, haptic feedback, and psychoacoustics to build non-visual feedback that people notice when it matters and can ignore when it doesn't, balancing urgency, clarity, and calm.

I build the rigs, run the studies, and ship the findings: multichannel audio and XR prototypes in Max/MSP, Unity, and Unreal, evaluated through IRB-approved studies on noticeability, reaction time, and cognitive load. PhD in Human Factors at Virginia Tech; dissertation on immersive audio in extended reality, defending December 2026.
